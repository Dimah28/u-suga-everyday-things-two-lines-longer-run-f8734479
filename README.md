Entry for the "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run" bounty ($2) on [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479).

## The entry

> A toaster is like a tiny sun trapped in a metal box that browns your bread the way sunshine browns your skin at the beach.
> The sun part maps the heat that changes colour, but it leaves out the timer and the spring that pops the bread up before it burns.

MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
